THE COMPANY

Rooted in
Biella.

Founded in 1946 in Tollegno, in the heart of the Biella textile district, Filatura Cervinia has built its identity around yarn manufacturing and textile expertise.

The production process follows the yarn through multiple stages, from the preparation of raw materials to the finished product.

Decades of experience meet a modern approach to production, quality and international markets.
